Police & Criminal Matters
Since 1 July 2024, India's criminal law runs on three new codes: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA). They replaced the IPC, the CrPC and the Indian Evidence Act.
