Tenancy & Property

Most tenancy problems come down to what the agreement says, what notice was given, and what proof you have. Constitution Ai helps you read your rent agreement, understand your rights as a tenant or landlord, and plan the next step, from a polite reminder to a legal notice or a civil suit.

It also explains property paperwork in plain words: what a sale deed or lease needs, when a document must be registered, and which questions to ask before you sign.

Security depositsEviction noticesRent agreements

Key laws

Transfer of Property Act, 1882

General rules on leases, including notice to end a tenancy (Section 106).

State rent control laws

For example the Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 or the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999.

Model Tenancy Act, 2021

A model law that applies only where a state has adopted it.

Registration Act, 1908

Leases for more than one year must be registered (Section 17).

What it covers

  • Getting a security deposit back
  • Eviction and notice periods
  • Rent increases and repairs
  • Registering leases and sale deeds
