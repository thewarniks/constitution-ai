Our Mission

Most people meet the legal system at a stressful moment: a notice from a landlord, a refund that never came, a police station that won't listen. Constitution Ai exists to make that first step less confusing. It explains the law in plain words, shows the options and deadlines, and helps draft the letters people need, while being honest that it is not a lawyer and pointing to free legal aid and professional help when it matters.

Principles
How We Build It
Plain Language First

If a 15-year-old can't follow the answer, it isn't finished. Jargon is explained, not assumed.

Honest About Limits

It never invents case citations, says when the law is unclear, and recommends a lawyer for serious matters.

Current Law

Instructions are kept up to date with major changes, like India's move to the BNS, BNSS and BSA in 2024.

Privacy Respected

Chats are stored in your browser, not on our server, and API keys never reach the page.

Access for Everyone

Free AI engines, ten languages, voice input, and links to free legal aid when you need a real lawyer.

Open by Design

Bring your own AI provider and your own reference documents. Nothing is locked to one vendor.
